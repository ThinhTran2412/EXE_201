# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- Customers and photographers using a mobile app to discover, chat, book, and complete photo sessions.
- The current surface in focus is the photographer booking workspace, where photographers need to review upcoming jobs, act on pending bookings, and understand their schedule at a glance.

## Product Purpose
ShootMatch helps customers and photographers connect around photo sessions, turn matches into bookings, manage conversations, and complete shoots with clear status tracking. Success means users can understand what is happening, what needs attention, and what to do next without confusion.

## Brand Personality
Confident, editorial, practical.
The product should feel polished and premium, but still direct and efficient for everyday task completion.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with flat gray tables and weak hierarchy.
- Overly decorative UI that hides status and action clarity.
- Empty card grids where every block looks identical.
- Interfaces that bury the primary actions behind visual noise.

## Design Principles
- Make state legible first, style second.
- Prioritize the next action for the current role.
- Use editorial framing to elevate the product without sacrificing speed.
- Separate overview, calendar context, and actionable bookings clearly.
- Keep content dense enough to be useful, but not cramped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Aim for WCAG AA contrast.
- Preserve readable hierarchy on smaller phones.
- Keep tap targets large and spaced enough for fast booking actions.
- Avoid motion that blocks comprehension or creates unnecessary friction.
